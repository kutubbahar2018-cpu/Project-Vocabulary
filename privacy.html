Privacy

Posh is a free vocabulary app. This page explains what it stores and what it doesn't.

What is stored, and where

Your progress, favourite words, quiz results and settings are saved only in your own browser, under the name you choose. Nothing is sent to a server.

Clearing your browser's site data, using a private tab, or switching to another browser or device starts fresh. To keep your progress, use Back up progress in Settings and keep the file.

Anonymous page counts

The app counts page views with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't collect names or learner data, and runs only on the published website.

Backup files

A backup file contains your progress only. It isn't encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe. Restoring a file replaces the progress in the browser you restore it in.
